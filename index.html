How it works

From detection to help, in four steps

Built to let you cancel a false alarm, and to make sure a real one is never missed.

1

Detect

A fall plus no movement, or critical vitals, starts the alert logic.

2

Confirm

The band vibrates. The wearer can cancel within seconds. No response is treated as real.

3

Alert

Emergency contacts and your ambulance number receive your vitals and a location link.

4

Track

Location and vitals keep updating until someone acknowledges.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is H Band a medical device?

No. It is a safety and monitoring aid. It does not diagnose conditions or replace professional medical care.

When will glucose monitoring be available?

It is still in development, so we have no release date yet. Message us on WhatsApp and we will tell you as soon as it is ready. Pre-order customers hear first.

Who gets the emergency alert?

The emergency contacts you add, plus an ambulance or emergency number you choose. Each alert carries your vitals and a map link to your location.

What if it triggers by mistake?

The band vibrates first, and you can cancel within seconds. Alerts only go out if there is no response.
